🚀 Quick Start

1

Navigate to Analyzer

Click on the "Analyzer" tab to start analyzing your music files.

2

Select Your Music Folder

Choose the directory containing your music files using the browse button or enter the path manually.

3

Start Analysis

Click "Start Analysis" to begin processing. Tracks will be analyzed and added to your library automatically.

4

Manage Your Library

Use the "Libraries" tab to filter tracks, view statistics, and generate smart playlists.

5

Create & Play Playlists

Generate playlists with harmonic mixing, save them, and play directly in the built-in audio player.
